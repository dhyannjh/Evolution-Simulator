#Evolution Simulator
A Python simulation exploring natural selection, reproduction, survival, and genetic mutation.
##About
The simulation contains a population of entities that move around a world and search for food to survive.
Entities have inherited traits such as:
* Speed
* Maximum energy
* Reproduction rate
* Color
When entities reproduce, their offspring inherit these traits with small random mutations.
Over time, environmental pressures such as food availability can cause certain traits to become more common in the population.
##Current Features
* Entity movement
* Food spawning
* Energy and survival system
* Collision-based food collection
* Reproduction
* Genetic mutations
* Reproduction cooldown
* Population tracking
##Built With
* Python
* Pygame
* Matplotlib (planned for simulation data visualization)
##Goal
The long-term goal is to visualize how a population changes over multiple generations and investigate whether traits that improve survival become more common over time.
